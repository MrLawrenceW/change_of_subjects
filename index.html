Formula Sheet (Levels 1–3)

  • Remove brackets: \(a(b+c) = ab+ac\)
  • Cross multiply: \(\dfrac{a}{b}=\dfrac{c}{d} \Rightarrow ad=bc\)
  • Move subject terms: collect all subject terms on one side
  • H.C.F.: common factors with LOWEST power
  • L.C.M.: all factors with HIGHEST power
  • Fractions: \(\dfrac{a}{b}\pm\dfrac{c}{d}=\dfrac{ad\pm bc}{bd}\)
  • Difference of squares: \(a^2-b^2=(a+b)(a-b)\)
  • Identity expansion: \((x+a)(x+b)=x^2+(a+b)x+ab\)
  • Perfect square: \((x\pm a)^2=x^2\pm 2ax+a^2\)
  • Compare coefficients: coefficients of each power of \(x\) are equal
